Add opt-in, anonymous usage telemetry, and say plainly what leaves your Mac.

**Nothing is sent unless you say yes.** After installing (or updating), the app asks once whether to share anonymous usage data, and you can change your answer in Settings → About. If you opt in, it reports app launches, whether recordings and model loads worked (with recording length and loudness, never content), crash types, and your app version, macOS version and model. It never sends your audio, transcripts, selected text, vocabulary or which apps you use. The README has a new Privacy section listing every event.
