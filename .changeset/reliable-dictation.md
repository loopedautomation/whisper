Make dictation more dependable. Recording carries on for a moment after you release the key, so the last word isn't cut off. Silent recordings are dropped instead of being transcribed into stray phrases, and a key that was only brushed no longer starts a dictation. Your clipboard, including images and files, comes back after every paste. Your dictations are kept in a history on your Mac, with a shortcut to paste the last one again. Correct a past dictation in Settings → History and the app learns from it: the fix is applied to future dictations, names join your vocabulary, and the rewrite model sees it. Settings save as you change them, and the menu opens with a link to Looped. Realtime mode and its live caption are removed: dictation is transcribed once, when you stop. Usage telemetry is removed entirely: the app no longer asks to share anything, and sends nothing.
